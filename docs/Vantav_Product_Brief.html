Vantav Commerce OS — Beta Product Brief

Vantav

The AI operations platform for Gen Z and small-business merchants selling across Shopify, TikTok Shop, Amazon, and beyond.

What Vantav does

Vantav replaces spreadsheet chaos and channel-specific dashboards with one place to see true profit, catch problems early, and act on recommendations you control.

  • Unified channels: Connect Shopify, TikTok Shop, Amazon, and eBay into a single sales and inventory view.
  • True profit: Net profit after COGS, shipping, marketplace fees, payment processing, ad spend, refunds, and operating costs.
  • Attention alerts: Margin compression, refund spikes, stockouts, and runway issues surface automatically.
  • Recommended actions: AI-generated drafts with confidence scores, evidence, and projected impact.
  • Approval gate: Nothing executes in a live store until the merchant approves, modifies, or dismisses it.

How it works

Workflow: Ingest → True Profit → Detect → Evidence → Recommend → Approve → Execute → Verify → Learn

Data from every storefront, ad account, supplier, and cost feed is normalized into a single ledger. The system calculates contribution profit, runs forecasting and rules checks, and drafts actions with evidence. The merchant sees the recommendation, the expected impact, and the reasoning, then decides what happens next.

Why it stands out

  • Channel-agnostic: Shopify Sidekick and most competitors are tied to one platform. Vantav works across all major channels.
  • Profit-first: Most dashboards lead with revenue. Vantav leads with net earnings and margin, so decisions protect cash.
  • Merchant-in-control AI: Every recommendation is a draft, not a command. The approval gate keeps the human in the loop.
  • Built for lean operations: Small teams get an operations layer that normally requires a CFO, logistics manager, and ad buyer.

Target market

Gen Z entrepreneurs and small-business owners running multi-channel commerce with thin margins and no time to reconcile spreadsheets. They need one command center that tells them what is happening, what needs attention, and what to approve next.

Beta readiness

  • Three-pillar overview: True Profit, Attention Alerts, Recommended Actions.
  • Action compliance card with confidence, evidence, and approve/dismiss controls.
  • Floating AI Assistant widget with page-specific recommendations on all dashboard pages except Overview.
  • Performance benchmarks passed: rules engine ~9.6 µs per SKU, COO diagnostic ~0.013 ms per SKU.
  • Production hardening: security headers, request metrics, database/storage health checks, and SLA alerting.
  • Live monitoring dashboard with P95 latency, error rate, channel sync age, and alert dispatch.

Billing & subscriptions

The dashboard Billing page now shows the active plan, real usage against tier limits, accrued invoice balance, and one-click access to the Stripe Customer Portal. New merchants can subscribe through a public checkout page, and upgrades are handled by Stripe Checkout with webhook tier synchronization.

Live channel write-backs

Approved actions are no longer local-only. A dedicated outbound layer attempts real updates to connected marketplaces: Shopify inventory and price updates, TikTok Shop and Amazon inventory queues, and ad budget targets. Missing credentials are skipped safely and logged.
