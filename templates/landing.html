Questions

Straight answers

Which channels can I connect?

Shopify, TikTok Shop and Amazon today, with Stripe for payments. If you sell somewhere else, tell us — we add channels based on what customers actually need.

What exactly does "true profit" include?

Revenue minus product cost, marketplace and payment fees, shipping and fulfilment, discounts, refunds, and the ad spend attributed to those sales. It is the number left in your bank account, not gross sales.

Will Vantav change anything in my store on its own?

No. It drafts the recommendation and shows you the numbers behind it. Nothing is paused, reordered or repriced until you approve it, and you can edit or dismiss any suggestion.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Create an account and you get a full workspace to explore with your own connected data before you commit to a plan.

How do you handle my store credentials?

You connect through each platform's own authorisation screen — we never ask for your store password. Access is read-only for reporting, tokens are encrypted, and you can disconnect a channel at any time.

How long does setup take?

Connecting a channel takes a few minutes. Most stores are looking at accurate profit the same afternoon; the only manual part is entering product costs, and you can import those from a spreadsheet.

Do I need an analyst or a developer?

No. Vantav is built for the owner or operator. If you can read your Shopify dashboard, you can run Vantav — and you can always just ask the assistant.
