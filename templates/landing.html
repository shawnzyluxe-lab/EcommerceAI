FAQ

Questions merchants ask first

Which channels does Vantav support?

Shopify, TikTok Shop, and Amazon today, with Stripe for payments. Costs, fees, and ad spend from those channels feed the same profit engine, so numbers are comparable across all of them.

How is "true profit" different from what Shopify shows me?

Shopify reports revenue and a limited cost view. Vantav subtracts marketplace and payment fees, shipping, discounts, refunds and returns, COGS, and channel ad spend — so the number you see is what lands in the bank, broken down by channel and SKU.

Does Vantav change anything in my store on its own?

No. Every recommendation — pausing an ad set, reordering stock, changing a price — arrives as an action with its supporting numbers. Nothing executes until you approve it, and you can edit or skip any suggestion.

Can I try it before connecting my stores?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 48-hour sandbox using sample data, so you can walk the full dashboard before authorizing any channel.

How are my credentials handled?

API credentials are encrypted at rest, scoped to the permissions each integration requires, and never shared between merchants. See our security page for details.

How long does setup take?

Most merchants connect their channels and see a populated dashboard the same afternoon. Historical orders backfill in the background.
